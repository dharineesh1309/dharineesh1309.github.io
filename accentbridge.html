accentbridge

indian-to-us accent conversion, live on a video call. you talk normally; the other side hears a us accent, about 200 ms later.

turn your sound on. the demo is a recorded call with the converter running live.

~10Mparameters
20 msstreaming frames
~200 mslatency on a plain cpu
onnxruntime, no gpu
the hard part

there is no dataset for this.

to learn an accent, a model needs the same sentence, said by the same voice, in both accents, thousands of times over. nobody has recorded that. not at scale, not in public, not anywhere.

how it works

a small streaming model listens in 20 ms chunks, maps indian-accented speech to a us accent, and a hifi-gan vocoder turns it back into audio, all fast enough to sit inside a live call.

it keeps the speaker's own voice and is exported to onnx runtime, so it runs on an ordinary laptop cpu.
